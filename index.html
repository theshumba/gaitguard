The Problem

Mild lameness is invisible to the human eye

Vets only agree 62% of the time when assessing subtle lameness. By the time you can see it, the damage is already done.

The Eye Isn't Enough

The human eye cannot detect gait asymmetries below 3-5mm. Subtle lameness gets missed, even by the most experienced people in the yard.

Gaps Between Checks

Vet assessments happen periodically. A horse can develop problems and worsen between visits. What happens on the days nobody is watching?

Caught Too Late

By the time you notice lameness, the condition has already progressed. Early intervention saves months of rehabilitation and thousands in lost potential.

How It Works

Set it once. Monitor every stride.

A camera at your horse walker captures every horse, every session. The AI analyses movement and flags changes. You don't touch a thing.

Monitoring

What GaitGuard tracks

Continuous biomechanical analysis across multiple gait parameters, compared against each horse's own baseline.

Gait Asymmetry

Vertical head and pelvic displacement measured to sub-millimetre precision. Forelimb and hindlimb lameness differentiated automatically.

Stride Regularity

Stride length, duration, and stance/swing phase timing tracked across every session. Irregularities flagged against historical data.

Trend Detection

Gradual changes over days and weeks that are impossible to see with the eye. A horse becoming 2% more asymmetric each week is caught early.

Individual Baselines

Every horse has a unique movement signature. GaitGuard learns each horse's normal gait and alerts only when something genuinely changes.
